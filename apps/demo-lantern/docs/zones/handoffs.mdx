# Handoffs

A <Terminology title="Lets a trusted person disarm without your phone" to="/docs/terminology#handoff">handoff</Terminology> lets a trusted person <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">disarm</Terminology> a <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology> without your phone in their hand. <Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology> accepts the pass, records who held it, and lets the lights stay on.

## Handoff types

- **By name** — the household roster recognizes the voice
- **By passphrase** — a soft phrase, set in advance
- **By <Terminology title="Apple hub Lantern pairs with during setup" to="/docs/terminology#homekit-home-hub">Home Hub</Terminology> presence** — the hub already trusts this person
- **By schedule** — a window of trust that opens and closes itself

## When the handoff arrives

```bash
lantern handoff list
```

Every <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">disarm</Terminology> becomes a line in the <Terminology title="Record every motion and arm/disarm change passes through" to="/docs/terminology#event-stream">event stream</Terminology>. The lantern remembers who came home.
