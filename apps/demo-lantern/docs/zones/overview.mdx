# Zones

A <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology> is a named room, hallway, exterior, or appliance group that <Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology> <Terminology title="General term for an active monitoring session" to="/docs/terminology#watch">watches</Terminology> together. Group your devices by where they live, not by what they are.

## Default zones

- **Perimeter** — exterior doors, windows, gates
- **Interior** — hallways, stairwells, common rooms
- **<Terminology title="Default zone covering sleeping areas after dark" to="/docs/terminology#watch">Watch</Terminology>** — sleeping areas, after dark
- **Quiet** — bedrooms, bathrooms — <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">armed</Terminology> only on <Terminology title="Tightened rule set entered for a fixed date range" to="/docs/terminology#travel-mode">travel mode</Terminology>

## Arming a zone

<Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">Arm</Terminology> any <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology> independently. <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">Disarm</Terminology> gently, by name. Override with a phrase. <Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology> never raises its voice.
