# Welcome to Lantern

<Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology> is a whole-house monitoring toolkit for HomeKit hubs that prefer warm light to flashing red.

The whole house, gently held. From the front porch to the back hallway, every <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology> is <Terminology title="General term for an active monitoring session" to="/docs/terminology#watch">watched</Terminology> without ever feeling <Terminology title="General term for an active monitoring session" to="/docs/terminology#watch">watched</Terminology>.

## What you can do

- Discover and <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">arm</Terminology> devices in any <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology>
- Stream motion events as they happen
- Browse <Terminology title="Queryable ledger of every recorded motion and change" to="/docs/terminology#sensor-history">sensor history</Terminology> without leaving the room
- <Terminology title="Lets a trusted person disarm without your phone" to="/docs/terminology#handoff">Hand off</Terminology> <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">arm/disarm</Terminology> to someone you trust at 2am

There is nothing to configure that you would not want a porch light to know.
