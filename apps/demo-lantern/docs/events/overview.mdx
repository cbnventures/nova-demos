# Events

Every motion, contact, and <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">arm/disarm</Terminology> passes through <Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology>'s <Terminology title="Record every motion and arm/disarm change passes through" to="/docs/terminology#event-stream">event stream</Terminology>. Replay, filter, hand off to a hub. Nothing is dropped, nothing is shouted.

## Event types

- **Motion** — directional, with an optional <Terminology title="Directional certainty attached to a motion event" to="/docs/terminology#confidence-band">confidence band</Terminology>
- **Contact** — door or window opened, closed, held
- **<Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">Arm / disarm</Terminology>** — by <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology>, by user, by schedule
- **<Terminology title="Queryable ledger of every recorded motion and change" to="/docs/terminology#sensor-history">Sensor history</Terminology>** — a quiet ledger you can read at noon
