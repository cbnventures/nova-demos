# Sensor History

<Terminology title="Queryable ledger of every recorded motion and change" to="/docs/terminology#sensor-history">Sensor history</Terminology> is the quiet ledger of every motion, contact, and <Terminology title="Named area Lantern arms, disarms, and watches together" to="/docs/terminology#zone">zone</Terminology> change <Terminology title="Whole-house monitoring toolkit signaling with warm light" to="/docs/terminology#lantern">Lantern</Terminology> has seen. Read it at noon. Read it after a long weekend. The lantern keeps the page open.

## What history captures

- **Motion arcs** — direction, confidence, time of day
- **Contact dwell** — how long the back door was held
- **<Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">Arm and disarm</Terminology> cadence** — who <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">armed</Terminology> when, who <Terminology title="Turning a zone's monitoring on or off" to="/docs/terminology#arm--disarm">disarmed</Terminology> when
- **Quiet stretches** — the spaces between

## Reading the ledger

```bash
lantern history --zone perimeter --since 7d
```

The output is a single column of times and gentle verbs. Nothing is summarized away.
